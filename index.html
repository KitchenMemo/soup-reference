Quick reference

  • FormulaAromatics → main ingredients → liquid → adjust body → season in stages → finish with acid/herbs
  • Poultry & reheating165°F (74°C) internal; bring soups to a rolling boil when reheating
  • CoolingRefrigerate or freeze within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C), in shallow containers
  • Fridge / freezer3–4 days at 40°F (4°C) or below; best quality 2–3 months frozen
  • Too salty?Dilute with unsalted liquid or ingredients. Acid and potato do not remove sodium
  • Rule of thumbOne change at a time, then taste or check texture again

Cooling, storing and reheating

Based on USDA food-safety guidance. Freezer times are quality guidance; continuously frozen food stays safe indefinitely.

StageGuidanceContainer / method
CoolingRefrigerate or freeze within 2 hours; within 1 hour if the room is above 90°F (32°C)Shallow containers; do not leave a big pot out to cool
RefrigerationUse within 3–4 days; fridge at 40°F (4°C) or belowShallow, covered containers
FreezingSoups and stews: about 2–3 months for best qualityFreezer-safe airtight containers; leave room for expansion; label with date
ThawingThaw in the refrigerator or by a safe microwave methodKeep portions sealed in the fridge
ReheatingLeftovers to 165°F (74°C); soups, sauces and gravies to a rolling boilHeat evenly, stir, check temperature
SpoilageDiscard soup past safe time limits or showing spoilage signsNever taste questionable food to test it

Freezes well vs. freezes badly

Results depend on ingredients, preparation and reheating. When texture matters, freeze the base and add delicate parts after thawing.
